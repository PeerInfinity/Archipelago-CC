# Hero Core

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export
a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Hero Core is a freeware action-exploration shooter by Daniel Remar (Remar Games, 2010). The randomizer turns these
into locations, each of which can hold any item:

- destroying generators
- the Blade, Suit, Blaster and Pattern Scanner powerups dropped by bosses
- level ups dropped by bosses
- computers
- doors that open once every enemy in a room is defeated, and battle doors
- save points

The goal is one of the game's three difficulties (Normal, Hard, Annihilation), optionally requiring up to 10
computers before the final boss.

## What items can appear in other players' worlds?

Blade, Suit and Blaster upgrades, the Pattern Scanner, level ups and computers.

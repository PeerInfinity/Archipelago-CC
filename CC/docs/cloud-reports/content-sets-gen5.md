72.3 s):

**The loop/step delta: 0.** The sphere log is byte-identical, so the bot's legs are the same 25 (9 checks plus 16
routing hops). Within the rooms, every endpoint-to-endpoint autopath (`findPath`) between the entrance, each exit and
each location was measured before and after: **44 legs, 456 steps before, 456 after, 0 longer, 0 unreachable.**
The row's time moved by 1 s, inside GEN-2's spread for run-to-run noise.

**No bot leg crosses a ledge in this seed.** Of those 44 shortest walks, none passes through a ledge cell. That is the
law working: (c) holds the gate-aware walk equal to the room's before the kind, so a ledge can sit on a shortest
route only where it costs nothing in either direction. In these open 16×12 rooms, BFS's ties always went round it.

**A walk that crosses one, in the committed rooms.** For each ledge, from its north neighbour to its south neighbour
(16 of 17; BlackCastle (11,11) sits on the bottom row):
- `findPath` goes **through the ledge** in 2 steps, e.g. Overworld `(11,6) → (11,7) → (11,8)`: entered moving S, the
  allowed direction;
- the same walk back, S → N, takes **4 steps**: the move N onto the ledge is shut, so the autopather goes round it;
- `step` onto every ledge: moving S enters; moving N, E or W from a floor neighbour returns null (pinned, § 3).

## 9. What the code overturned, and the next slice's line

1. **The brief expected a mid-run crossing; this seed has none.** The bot's walks never step on a ledge: law (c)
   holds every room's gate-aware walk, so a ledge only lands where a shortest route can go round it (§ 5). The
   crossing is shown on the committed rooms with the autopather and `step` instead, and pinned.
2. **GEN-2's lab gap (§ 5 there) no longer reproduces.** The lab loads a pipeline room whose sidecar records a region
   biome.
3. **Defects my own tests found.** None in the code. The art's first chevron was too thin to read at 1×. That was
   found by looking at the 1× shot, and fixed before commit.
4. **Standing artifacts.** `furniture`'s demo counts in the docs (`pipeline-presets.md`, the shipped preset's
   description) now read 13 rugs, 18 pillars, 17 ledges, not 4 and 4 per room.
5. **Next slice's line.**
   - **Per-placement `dirs`** (out here): a pack field letting the generator try each rotation the pack allows. The
     91 (a) refusals of § 6 are what it would recover. A design question for the replan.
   - **A demo where the bot crosses a ledge** would need a room where the ledge is on every shortest route in its
     allowed direction and costs nothing back. With law (c) as it stands, that is a corridor with a free parallel way
     of equal length. It would be a smaller or walled room, which GEN-2 § 7.2 found pulls against placing anything.

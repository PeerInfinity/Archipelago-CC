## D2: `tapeEnvelope.js` and its test

- `readEnvelope(raw)` returns a frozen `{game, tape_version, profile, boot, rng, tick_count, inputs}` for any game id.
  - It validates only what the envelope makes common: a non-empty `game`, an integer `tape_version` ≥ 1, the `{id, md5}` shape of a profile, finite `boot.x` and `boot.y`, `rng` as an object, spans as `{key, from, to}` with integer ticks and `0 <= from < to <= tick_count`, and `tick_count` defaulting to the longest span, as `parseTape` does.
  - Key vocabulary and overlap rules are left to each game.
- `inclusiveToHalfOpen([a,b])` returns `{from:a, to:b+1}`. `halfOpenToInclusive` is the reverse.
- `validateProfile` is exported. `tapeFormat`'s v13 field uses it, so the two readers share one definition of a profile.
- The test, `npx vitest run frontend/modules/seedlingDemo/tapeEnvelope.test.js`, passes **23 tests**:
  - all 154 committed tapes read, and agree with `parseTape` on version, tick_count, boot and the span set;
  - they also agree on the held-key set at every span edge;
  - a synthetic `robotkitty` Flash tape built from §1's field list reads, and `parseTape` still refuses it with `game must be "seedling"`;
  - 13 invalid inputs are each refused with a named error;
  - `[7,7]` becomes `{from:7, to:8}` and is held on tick 7 only.

## D3: the v13 `profile` field

- `SUPPORTED_TAPE_VERSIONS` gains 13, and a new `PROFILE_TAPE_VERSION = 13` is added.
- A tape below 13 that declares a profile is a named error. The shape check goes through `validateProfile`.
- The field is **presence-scoped** (it follows `name`'s precedent and is not normalised to `null`), so an undeclared tape carries no `profile` key at all.
- `requiredTapeVersion` returns 13 first, outranking `hold`.
- `GAME_VISIBLE_DROPS` gains `'profile'`, and `gameVisibleTape` destructures it away. The projection rule is unchanged: `hold ? 12 : min(version, 8)`.
- `serializeTape` writes the field only when a v13 tape declares it.
- **Decision: `TAPE_VERSION` stays 12.** Six builders stamp that constant onto the tapes they build: `playthroughAcceptance.test.js`, `r7Acceptance.test.js`, and four probe/plan scripts under `scripts/procgen/`. Bumping it would re-version those tapes to 13 for a feature they don't use. The reasoning is in the docblock. The precedent (v9–v11 bumped it) pointed the other way. **⚖ Ruled by the user, 2026-09-30: *"I agree with your recommendations."* `TAPE_VERSION` stays 12.**

## D4: `observationTolerance.js` and its test

- `compareObservationStreams(expected, actual, {epsilon:{x,y}, transitionSlack})` returns `{agrees, mode, firstDivergence, maxDelta:{x,y,t}, divergentTicks, roomSequenceAgrees, transitions, length}`.
- A transition at `t` has the slack window `[t - slack, t + slack)`. That is exactly the set of ticks where a crossing moved by up to `slack` ticks shows the other room, and it is **empty at slack 0**. That emptiness is what makes exact mode provably equal to `diffObservationStreams`.
- `diffObservationStreams` is untouched.
- `npx vitest run frontend/modules/seedlingDemo/observationTolerance.test.js`: **108 tests passed**, 26.5 s wall.
  - Exact-mode equivalence tally: `{"recorded":154,"pass":150,"fail":4,"nudged":154,"late":44,"swapped":154}`.
  - The 4 disagreements are the known `EXPECTED_TO_DIVERGE` set, so both verdicts are exercised on the real roster.
  - "late" covers 44 of the 73 transition-bearing streams. The others are skipped by the guard that keeps the shifted record valid: a next transition one tick away, or the stream's end.
  - Tolerance rows: x+1e-9 agrees under ε 1e-6. A one-tick-late transition agrees at slack 1 but not at slack 0. A room swapped outside every window never agrees at slack 0 or 1, even under ε 1e9. A length difference of 1 is tolerated at slack 1; a difference of 2 is not.
  - The fence: no string literal naming the module appears in any `scripts/procgen/check-*.mjs` (the roster is read off disk), `tapeRunner.test.js`, `fixtures/index.js` or `watchWasm.js`. Files are read as latin1 so a NUL byte cannot hide a match.

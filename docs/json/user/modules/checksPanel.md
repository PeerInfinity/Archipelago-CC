# Checks Panel

The Checks panel is one line: how far the loaded game has got.

> Checked: 3/24, Reachable: 10, Unreachable: 11, Events: 1/2

- **Checked** — locations checked, of the game's locations.
- **Reachable** / **Unreachable** — the unchecked locations you can reach now, and the rest.
- **Events** — event locations collected, of all of them.

Hover the line for the same numbers in words. The [Console](../standard-client.md) shows the same line.

The panel is meant for the **mini row**: turn **Checks** on in the [Quick Launch](quickLaunch.md) panel's **Mini panels** section and it appears as a thin row above the middle column. When the [Playback Bot](playbackBot.md) is mini too, the line moves into the bot's mini row and this panel is taken out until the bot is expanded again.

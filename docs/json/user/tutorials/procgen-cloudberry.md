# Procgen: a Cloudberry Kingdom world

The **Cloudberry Kingdom** substrate turns each region into a level made by Cloudberry Kingdom's own level generator. A level is a row of pieces, and each piece adds a kind of platform — moving blocks, clouds, elevators and so on. Until you hold a kind's item its platforms are drawn dashed and you fall through them.

Some coins — drawn as the Archipelago logo — are the region's locations. Its exits are all at the **end door**: press **Up** there for a menu of the exits, one per side. The **start door** offers the way back.

Cloudberry levels are generated by the game page itself, which takes a while, so this tutorial plays a world that is already made.

The game is about 26 MB. It is loaded when the first Cloudberry level opens, so the first level can take a few seconds to appear.

## Watch it play

1. Load **Cloudberry Sphere WorldGen** (seed 1) — in the **Presets** panel, or by opening the page with `?game=cloudberry_sphere_worldgen&seed=1`. **Do it** loads it for you.
2. Open the **Cloudberry Kingdom** tab: the first level. Click into it and use the **arrow keys** to run and jump yourself; **Up** at a door opens its menu.
3. Open the **Playback Bot** tab. It says "Sphere log loaded" — it has the world's solution path, sphere by sphere.
4. Press **▶** (Play), then open the **Cloudberry Kingdom** tab to watch the bot play each level to its check coins, then out by the end door, collect each sphere's items and finish.
